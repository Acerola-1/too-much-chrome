Too Much Chrome

See how many web-engine apps are hiding on your Mac.

Too Much Chrome scans every Chromium / WebView app on your macOS — Electron, CEF, NW.js, Tauri, Wails, and full browsers like Chrome, Edge, and Brave — then measures how much storage they take and how current their engines are.

The name is a meme. The scan is dead serious.

HighlightsOne scan. The full picture.

Real Scanning

Every app in /Applications and your user folder, examined one by one. Even a renamed Electron build is recognized precisely — by its Bundle ID fingerprint.

Storage Statistics

Beyond the app itself, caches and data tucked away in ~/Library are counted too. Aggregated, deduplicated, and perfectly clear.

Version Health

Compared in real time against the latest official releases of each framework, rated in five tiers from green to red. Without a network, it falls back to built-in baselines.

Overview & Drill-down

Totals, footprint, distribution, and rankings — all in a single panel. Select any app to explore its storage breakdown and install path, or reveal it in Finder.

Swift Native

Crafted in SwiftUI. The scan runs on a background thread and exits when done — no resident process. On macOS 26, Liquid Glass arrives automatically.

Results Stay on Your Mac

No sign-in. No ads. No collection of any kind. The only online requests check for the latest framework versions — your scan results never leave your Mac.

Detection TiersIdentification confidence varies by framework — and every tier is labeled as it is

Tier 1 · High accuracy

Electron / CEF / NW.js

Identified by a dual fingerprint of framework directory name and Bundle ID, with virtually no misjudgments. Even a renamed framework (e.g. QQNT.framework) cannot hide the com.github.Electron identifier in its Bundle ID.

Tier 2 · Experimental

Tauri / Wails

Neither leaves an obvious framework fingerprint, so they are inferred from Bundle IDs, resource directories, and build-path signatures inside the binary. Wails builds obfuscated with garble remain undetectable — a known limitation, honestly labeled as experimental.

Tier 3 · Listed only

Full Browsers

Browsers such as Chrome, Edge, and Brave are listed as-is, with versions rated against the Chromium baseline. Divergent version schemes are resolved through the UA string embedded in the binary.
